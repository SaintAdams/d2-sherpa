Welcome to D2 Sherpa

D2 Sherpa connects Destiny 2 players who want help with raids to experienced players who enjoy teaching them. It's free to use.

How it works

  1. Enter your Bungie ID in the box at the top right. The site remembers it for next time.
  2. Need help? Choose Requests from the menu and tell us which raid you want to learn and when suits you.
  3. Want to teach? Choose Open Requests, find one you'd like to help with and accept it.
  4. Get together. Message each other on Bungie or Discord to arrange the run, and find it any time under My Runs. Once you've played it, mark it as completed there and rate each other.

Ratings stay hidden until you've both rated, and the best sherpas climb the rankings under My Profile.
